Detailed Description

Blends neighboring ranges whose surface normals align.

Computes local normals from cartesian coordinates, then blends each range with its 4-neighbors when the angular difference is within max_angle_deg. Zero ranges are treated as missing and skipped in both normal estimation and blending. Normal computation uses forward differences; edges without full neighbors are left untouched.
