Detailed Description

Smooths ranges that deviate from a local planar neighborhood.

Computes a neighborhood mean within a square window and pulls outliers (beyond plane_threshold * base_range_mm) toward the local average. Assumes zero ranges are invalid and excluded from the neighborhood statistics.
