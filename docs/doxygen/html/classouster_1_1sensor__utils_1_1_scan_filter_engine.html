Detailed Description

Engine that applies scan-level filters and updates annotations.

The engine owns no state; it simply mutates the provided scan and annotation vectors in-place, keeping them in sync as scans are removed or modified. Removal preserves order of kept scans. Annotations are dropped along with their corresponding scans.
